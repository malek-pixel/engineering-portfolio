# MATLAB Work

## Overview

Engineering-computing exercises and self-directed practice in MATLAB, built while completing the **MATLAB Onramp** certification and continuing independently afterward. Focus areas include numerical methods, vectorized computation, and basic data visualization: foundational skills for vehicle performance and engineering analysis work.

## Focus Areas

Core areas covered so far: MATLAB fundamentals (variables, vectors, matrices, and scripts), plotting and visualizing numerical data, writing and debugging functions, and applying numerical methods to simple engineering problems.
